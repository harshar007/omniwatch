## ⚡ Why Support Omnitrix Watch?

Omnitrix Watch is an independent, 100% free and open-source project dedicated to the smartwatch research community. We believe in:
- 🟢 **Zero Paywalls:** Every feature, UI theme, audio synthesizer, and watch face generator is completely free.
- 🛡️ **Safety & Hardware Preservation:** Protecting wearable hardware from destructive commands and bricking.
- 🌌 **Nostalgia & Innovation:** Bringing the Ben 10 universe to life on real hardware with state-of-the-art Python & BLE engineering.

Your donations help us acquire test devices (smartwatches, Bluetooth analyzers, BLE sniffers), maintain CI/CD build infrastructure, and devote more time to reverse-engineering protocols.
